Is this catalogue entry real? (fragments of large galaxies)

There is no fragment flag, and blended_prob, extended_prob, extended_flag and she_flag are empty (NULL) in DR1. We measured how well the filled columns pick out the fragments of NGC 1483. The method compares the entry density with the field density and asks what fraction of the excess each column flags, and at what cost in real field sources.

det_quality_flag bit 512marks the whole extended-object area, real and fake entries alike
  • Wide: 525 excess entries at 40″–100″ (+58%). Deep: 1244 (+75%), with the galaxy itself split into 38 pieces. These are lower limits, since the disc also hides real background sources.
  • parent_id groups the deblended pieces of one parent. It is local to each tile, so match on (tile_index, parent_id). Most outer-disc fragments are independent detections (parent_id = -1).
  • Practical rule: define the galaxy region on the image (or by bit 512), treat entries inside it as unreliable, and correct counts statistically with the field density. Measure the galaxy itself on the mosaic.
  • Families can also be artefacts: in the same tile, a 17-entry "galaxy" is the halo of a saturated star plus a detector artefact.
