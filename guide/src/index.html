Using Euclid DR1 for extended galaxies

A practical guide to the Euclid Data Release 1 (DR1-Foundation, public on 12 November 2026). It covers what was released, how the data are organised, how to get cutouts, files, catalogues and spectra (every recipe was run on release day), and what to watch out for in photometry, 2-D modelling and low-surface-brightness (LSB) work on low-to-intermediate-redshift galaxies. The detailed, searchable knowledge base is in kb/00_index.md.

The one thing to remember for LSB work The standard mosaics have had the sky subtracted twice at small scales. Light on scales above about 10″ (VIS) and 0.5′ (NIR) is partly removed. Only the second (MER) step can be undone: add BGMOD back. For anything above ~1′, rebuild from the calibrated frames (see LSB).
