Catalogue tables and keys

MER tables come in five groups: dr1.mer_catalogue_<group> with wide_survey (888 M rows), wide_mode, deep_survey, deep_mode and deep_visits. These are the only tables with coordinates. Morphology (mer_morphology_*), photo-z (phz_*_wide|deep_*), spectroscopic redshifts (spectro_*) and spectra (spectra_source_*, key source_id) all join on object_id. The full column list is in kb/data_model/tap/.
