Photo-z and spectra at low redshift

  • Photo-z (NNPZ on 2FWHM-aperture fluxes scaled to total): at IE < 24, outliers are 7–13% and NMAD 0.04–0.05. The COSMOS reference sample lacks z < 0.2 and very massive galaxies, so masses are biased low there. The north (no u band) is worse. For nearby extended galaxies, prefer external spectroscopic redshifts (e.g. DESI) and your own total photometry. Note that 251 of 256 galaxies with IE < 18 in a test tile have phys_param_flags = 1, so the usual = 0 cut removes almost every bright nearby galaxy.
  • Spectra come from the red grism only in the EWS (1.21–1.89 µm, R > 480; blue grism only in deep fields). SPE is optimised for Hα at 0.84 < z < 1.88: only 40–60% of redshifts are correct at 0.1 < z < 0.84, and < 10% at z < 0.1. At low z the usable lines are Paβ, He I 1.083 µm, Paγ, [Fe II] and [S III] 0.95 µm (z > 0.27).
  • Recommended SPE cuts: spe_rank = 0, high spe_z_prob, and cross-check with photo-z. Even so, see below.
