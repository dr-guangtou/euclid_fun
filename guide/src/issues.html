Top known issues

  1. LSB light is filtered out at the MER stage; per-exposure skies are baked into mosaics and stacks.
  2. Large galaxies are shredded (NGC 1483: +58% entries around it in the wide catalogue, +75% in the deep), and no column isolates the fragments (see above). There are no special tiles for objects larger than 2′, and tile-edge truncation is possible.
  3. Disk+Sérsic and t_type columns are empty; Sérsic values in bands without data are initial guesses (helpdesk cleaning script).
  4. FLUX_RADIUS is limited to the segmentation area (biased low).
  5. MER PSF stamps are small and not renormalised (IE stamp sum ~0.96).
  6. T-PHOT bugs (recentring sign; numpy/FITS axis swap) mainly affect point sources.
  7. phz_classification codes are swapped in the TAP description (1 = star, 2 = galaxy).
  8. Mosaic footprints can be larger than the data; a few tiles have duplicate mosaics.
  9. astroquery PyPI 0.4.11 is not DR1-ready; sedm defaults return HTTP 401; SIA had no DR1 on release day.
  10. NIR astrometry is off by > 50 mas in 0.7% of the area; Pan-STARRS EXT flags were mishandled; avoid HSC r/i in favour of r2/i2.
